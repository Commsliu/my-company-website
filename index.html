01 — ABOUT US

One Discipline,
Every Stakeholder

Media, opinion, and policy move together now. LIU is built to work all three without treating any one as secondary.

Media, opinion, and policy don't move separately — a message built for only one of them rarely survives contact with the other two. In practice, that means communications isn't PR with a public affairs function bolted on — it's one discipline: the research that shapes a media narrative is the same research that shapes a policy position, and the audience insight that earns coverage is what tells you who needs to be reached inside a ministry. That is the discipline LIU is built around — Listen, Interpret, Usher.

L / LISTEN

Map the Frame Before Shaping It

Every stakeholder — journalist, regulator, the public — already holds a frame. We start by understanding it, not assuming it.

I / INTERPRET

Turn Signal into Position

U / USHER

Design the Choice, Don't Force It
